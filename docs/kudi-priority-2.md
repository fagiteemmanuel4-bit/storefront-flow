# Strap Priority 2 Upgrade

## Current scope

Priority 2 is focused on making Strap a daily operating system rather than a collection of isolated tools.

### Delivered in this phase

1. Global operational launchpad / Command Center
2. Notifications center
3. Activity and audit center
4. Business Insights workspace
5. Centralized capability/status model
6. Centralized Settings hub
7. Advanced Settings control surface with 30 configurable options
8. Staff creation as an interactive modal
9. Commerce Operations workspace covering inventory intelligence, variants, bundles, suppliers, purchase orders, stock transfers, discounts, loyalty, fulfillment and shipping
10. Temporary customer account/order-tracking architecture retained at 14 days
11. Store appearance authorization hardened to owner/manager boundaries
12. Wholesale / producer / retailer / dropshipper partner opportunity concept added as Coming Soon

## Intentionally held until tomorrow

- Online payments and subscription billing
- Custom domain verification and automated domain publishing
- Paid partner marketplace setup and onboarding

## Product positioning

Strap should not claim to be "better than Shopify" as a factual statement until independent production benchmarking supports it. The stronger public message is that Strap is building a focused retail operating system combining POS, inventory, online selling, staff controls, commerce operations and business intelligence in one workflow.

## Quality rule

A feature is not considered production-complete merely because its table or route exists. The final hardening pass must verify the full flow: UI -> authorization -> transaction -> error handling -> mobile behaviour -> deployment -> production verification.
