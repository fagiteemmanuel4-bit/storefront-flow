# Strap by Kryonara

Strap is a commercial retail operations platform for shops and growing retail businesses. It connects point of sale, product catalogue, inventory, customer records, staff, branches, expenses, reports and online orders in one workspace.

## Product

Strap helps retail owners and teams organise the work behind each sale, from product setup and checkout to stock management and online selling.

## Core capabilities

- Point of sale and transaction records
- Product catalogue and inventory management
- Customer records and staff roles
- Branch and location operations
- Expenses and business reporting
- Online storefront and order management
- Barcode scanning and receipt workflows on compatible devices
- Supported AI-client connections through the Strap MCP integration
